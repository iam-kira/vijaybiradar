Vijay Biradar Portfolio - Complete Summary
Vision

The goal is to create Vijay Biradar, a premium cinematic portfolio that feels like a film trailer, interactive story, and professional portfolio combined into one experience.

This is not intended to be a traditional resume website.

The visitor should feel like they are entering the world of:

A Data Engineer, AI Builder, Cybersecurity Advocate, Reader, Gamer, Rider, and Continuous Learner.
